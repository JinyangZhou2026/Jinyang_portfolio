Privacy & analytics

Your visit, your choice.

This portfolio uses optional analytics only after you choose “Allow analytics.” The site remains fully usable if you decline.

Who operates this website

This website is operated by Jinyang Zhou, a product and industrial designer based in Milan, Italy. For privacy questions, email jinyang.zhou2026@outlook.com.

What optional analytics measures

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics may process information such as pages visited, portfolio sections viewed, approximate location, device and browser information, referring source, and interaction timing. This website does not intentionally send your name, email address, or form contents to Google Analytics.

Why it is used

The information helps understand which projects and portfolio sections are useful, how visitors arrive at the site, and where the experience can be improved. The legal basis for this optional measurement is your consent.

Your choice

Analytics is off by default. Your choice is stored in your browser so the site can remember it. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using “Analytics settings” in the footer. Withdrawing consent removes this site’s Google Analytics cookies where the browser permits it and stops analytics from loading on future page views.

Analytics provider

Google Analytics is provided by Google. Google may process analytics data under its own terms and data-processing arrangements. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Analytics privacy information.

Updates

This notice was last updated on 12 August 2026 and may be revised if the website’s analytics setup changes.
